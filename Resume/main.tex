\section{Conferences and Publications}

\textbf{Agrawal, N.} (2026). Phase-Aware Deep Learning with Complex-Valued CNNs for Audio Signal Applications. \textit{IEEE Conference on Artificial Intelligence (CAI) 2026}, Granada, Spain.\\
{\small Proposed complex-valued CNN architectures and training adaptations (complex batch-norm, weight initialization, complex activations) to exploit phase information ignored by real-valued nets. Evaluated in three stages: image baselines, MFCC audio classification, and phase-aware GNNs. Results show that complex architectures can match real CNNs while offering improved handling of phase in audio pipelines \& highlight trade-offs in stability versus expressivity across different complex activations.}

\vspace{5pt}

\textbf{Agrawal, N.} Crisis, Compassion, and Capital: The Economics of Global Charitable Giving. \textit{Presented at the Singapore Economics Review Conference (SERC) 2026}, Singapore.\\
{\small Used the Russo-Ukraine war as a natural experiment and analyzed nearly 50,000 GlobalGiving projects across 201 countries to study whether major crises create new charitable capital or reallocate existing donor attention. DiD estimates show that 50-70\% of the surge in Ukraine-related giving reflects genuine additionality from new donors, while 30\% comes from substitution away from other causes, with emotionally less salient projects experiencing the largest crowding-out effects.}

\vspace{5pt}

\textbf{Agrawal, N.}, Shariffdeen, R., Wang, G., Rasnayaka, S., \& Iyer, G. N. (2025). Analysis of Student-LLM Interaction in a Software Engineering Project. \textit{IEEE/ACM International Workshop on Large Language Models for Code (LLM4Code), ICSE 2025}, Ottawa, Canada.\\
{\small Analyzed 13 weeks of student–LLM interaction, 730+ generated code snippets, and human intervention patterns to characterize how conversational interaction affects code generation quality and complexity. Results showed that interactive LLM use produced lower-complexity and higher-quality code than automated generation, identifying interaction design as an important determinant of LLM-assisted programming performance.}

\section{Working Papers}


\textbf{Agrawal, N.} MOSAIC: Set-Based Multi-Bit Watermarking for Language Models.\\
{\small Developed a novel set-based multi-bit watermarking framework that recovers embedded payloads from unordered token parity constraints using belief propagation. Demonstrated over 95\% exact payload recovery on Llama-2-7B for up to 32-bit messages while remaining robust to token reordering, insertion, and deletion without relying on model-specific decoding.}

\vspace{5pt}

\textbf{Agrawal, N.} Equilibrium Analysis for Strategic Information Revelation in Data Markets.\\
{\small Developed a tractable signalling model where data sellers choose how much of a dataset to disclose and buyers update beliefs accordingly. Derived conditions for pooling versus separating equilibria and used simulations to demonstrate how disclosure costs and prior beliefs shape market vulnerability to adverse selection. }

\vspace{5pt}

\textbf{Agrawal, N.} Crowding In or Crowding Out? Donor-Level Evidence on Private Charity and the Retreating State.\\
{\small Used the 2025 U.S. federal government shutdown as a natural experiment and analyzed 32.6 million GoFundMe donations to study how private charity responds to government withdrawal. Showed that new donor entry more than offsets individual-level substitution, but overall replacement remains economically negligible and highly unequal, with funding concentrated in wealthier states and campaign success depending more on deservingness narratives than on described material need.}

\vspace{5pt}


\textbf{Agrawal, N.} The Revealed Value of a Rare Bird: Travel-Cost Valuation of Biodiversity from Vagrant Bird Sightings.\\
{\small Developed a novel travel-cost framework using exogenous vagrant bird appearances and 13,271 eBird visits across India to estimate the recreational value of biodiversity. Identified the causal effect of rare species on birdwatching demand, recovering a revealed consumer surplus of approximately \$586 per rare-bird event. Distinguished participation from travel-distance responses and showed that rarity and novelty, rather than aesthetic appeal, drive recreational value.}

\vspace{5pt}

\textbf{Agrawal, N.} Rigidity as a Signal: A Game-Theoretic Explanation for Vacancies in Urban Real Estate Markets.\\
{\small Modeled vacancy as a deliberate, costly signal in a two-period game with heterogeneous sellers, deriving multiple equilibrium types including pooling-holdout, pooling-discount, and separating forms. Analyzed comparative statics and demonstrated how targeted policy levers such as vacancy taxes and price-ratio mandates can reshape equilibria towards more informative outcomes and improve allocative efficiency in urban housing markets.}
